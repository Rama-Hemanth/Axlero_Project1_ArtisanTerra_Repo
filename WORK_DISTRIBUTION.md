## 1. Team & Branch Allocation Rules
Per the **Axlero Project Execution Handbook V1.1**:
- **Solo Execution Mode**: If executing individually, all daily commits are pushed directly to the `main` branch (Section 7 & Section 9, Reminder #11).
- **Team Execution Mode**: Each active member creates and commits daily to their own dedicated branch (`feature/member-name`) before merging into `main` for Mid and Final Reviews (Section 7 & Section 8).

## 2. Communication & Unresponsive Member Protocol
- **Dedicated Channel**: Team Discord / WhatsApp group with a strict **<24-hour response SLA**.
- **48-Hour Unresponsive Rule**: Any member unresponsive for 48 hours is reported to the Axlero mentor with screenshot proof; remaining active members immediately continue committing daily to fulfill the mandatory **10-day Mid Review** and **20-day Final Review** commit thresholds without gaps.
